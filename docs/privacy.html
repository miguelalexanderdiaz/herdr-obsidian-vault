Privacy and trust

Herdr mods are trusted code. These two read your vault, never write into it, and send nothing from your notes anywhere.

In short

  • Network: one thing. A web image that a note shows is downloaded with curl when the note opens; the Web images button or remoteImages (Configuration) turns it off. No telemetry, no accounts.
  • Runs: programs you installed, none bundled: rg, node with a headless browser, Poppler, curl, the system opener, and small system tools that stamp files and find programs.
  • Reads: the selected vault, Obsidian’s vault list, and the Mermaid and MathJax inside your Obsidian app.
  • Writes: only data/vault in the Herdr home. Herdr’s shared.json keeps your vault paths after uninstall; delete its vault.* keys to forget them.

To check: search the mods for exec( to see every program they start; tests/render-e2e.test.mjs proves the render page fetches nothing.
